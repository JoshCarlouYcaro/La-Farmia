WELCOME TO LA FARMIA AGRINOVA

More than a farm.
A growing agribusiness.

La Farmia AgriNova is a growing agribusiness based in Bukidnon, Philippines. We are engaged in diversified agricultural enterprises, including corn production, banana farming intercropped with ginger, ube production, seedling nurseries, and livestock raising.

Driven by a passion for agriculture and a vision for a more productive and inclusive farming industry, we continue to develop our farm enterprises while creating opportunities for farmers, young agripreneurs, and local communities.

At La Farmia, we believe that agriculture is more than a business. It is a way to grow food, create livelihoods, and cultivate a better future.

02 / ABOUT US

Growing with purpose,
rooted in agriculture.

Who We Are

La Farmia AgriNova is an integrated and diversified agribusiness located in Bukidnon, Philippines. Our agricultural operations include crop production, banana and ube seedling propagation, and livestock raising.

We aim to develop a productive and sustainable agricultural enterprise through responsible farm management, diversification, continuous learning, and the adoption of appropriate agricultural technologies.

As we continue to grow, we aspire to contribute to the development of the agricultural sector by creating livelihood opportunities, supporting farmers, and inspiring more young people to participate in agriculture.

01

Our Vision

To become a recognized and innovative agribusiness that promotes productive, sustainable, and inclusive agriculture while creating meaningful opportunities for farmers and communities.

02

Our Mission

To produce quality agricultural products and planting materials, strengthen diversified farming enterprises, embrace agricultural innovation, and create livelihood opportunities that contribute to the growth of farming communities.

Angel Mae Bautista

03 / OUR FARMS

From the soil
to the herd.

Discover the diversified farm enterprises that make up La Farmia AgriNova.

01

Corn Farm

Our corn production enterprise focuses on growing maize for agricultural and commercial purposes. We apply practical farm management and production practices to support crop growth, harvest, and market delivery.

02

Banana & Ginger Farm

Our banana farm combines banana cultivation with ginger intercropping. This diversified production system allows us to make use of available farm space while cultivating different agricultural crops.

03

Ube Farm

Our ube farm is dedicated to the production of ube, a valuable agricultural crop with potential for food and market applications.

04

Banana Seedling Nursery

Our banana nursery offers planting materials for farmers and other customers interested in banana cultivation.
